# Privacy statement

Audio Summary is a set of instructions and one small script that Claude
follows on your behalf. Its author runs no server and collects no data from
you.

## What it processes

The recording you give Claude. The script transcribes it on the machine
Claude's shell runs on (your computer, or Claude's sandbox), with a Whisper
model downloaded once from the sherpa-onnx GitHub releases. The audio itself
is not uploaded anywhere by the script.

## Where data goes

- **The work folder** you or Claude choose: a raw `transcript.txt`. It stays
  until you delete it.
- **The document**: a Claude doc, or a Markdown file next to the recording.
- **Your Claude conversation**: the transcript text Claude reads to write the
  summary is part of that conversation and is handled under Anthropic's
  privacy policy and your Claude settings.

Nothing is sent anywhere else.

## The people in your recording

Recordings and transcripts hold other people's words. Get their consent to
record where the law requires it, keep the results private, and delete them
when asked.
